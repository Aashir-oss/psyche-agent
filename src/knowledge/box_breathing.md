ID: box_breathing
TITLE: Box Breathing
WHEN_TO_USE: When stress or anxiety raises your heart rate and you need to calm your nervous system quickly — before a difficult conversation, exam, or presentation.
STEPS:
- Sit comfortably and breathe out fully through your mouth.
- Breathe IN through your nose for 4 slow counts.
- HOLD your breath for 4 counts.
- Breathe OUT through your mouth for 4 counts.
- HOLD empty for 4 counts.
- Repeat for 4 rounds (about 2 minutes). Keep shoulders relaxed throughout.
SOURCE: Standard stress-management breathing technique used in stress-inoculation training; see Meichenbaum, D. — Stress Inoculation Training (book). Original summary written for this project.

TITLE: Sleep Hygiene Basics
WHEN_TO_USE: When poor sleep is worsening mood, anxiety, or focus — difficulty falling asleep, waking at night, or restless mornings.
STEPS:
- Keep a fixed wake-up time every day, including weekends — this anchors your body clock.
- Get daylight within an hour of waking (even 10 minutes outside helps).
- Stop caffeine after midday and avoid heavy meals close to bedtime.
- Make the bedroom dark, cool, and quiet; use the bed only for sleep.
- Put screens away 30–60 minutes before bed; read or stretch instead.
- If you can't sleep after ~20 minutes, get up, do something calm in dim light, then try again.
SOURCE: Adapted from standard CBT-I sleep hygiene guidance; see also NIMH — "Sleep" topic resources (U.S. public domain), https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics. Original summary written for this project.

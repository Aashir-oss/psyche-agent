TITLE: Behavioral Activation
WHEN_TO_USE: When low mood makes you withdraw and stop doing things, which then makes the mood worse — the classic depression trap of inactivity.
STEPS:
- List 10 activities you used to enjoy or that give a small sense of achievement (walk, cooking, calling a friend).
- Rate each 1–10 for pleasure and for mastery (sense of accomplishment).
- Schedule 2–3 of them into the next 3 days, at specific times, starting with the easiest.
- Do them even if motivation is low — action comes before motivation, not after.
- Afterwards, re-rate pleasure/mastery and notice any mood shift, however small.
- Build up gradually; add one activity per week.
SOURCE: Martell, C. R., Dimidjian, S., & Herman-Dunn, R. — Behavioral Activation for Depression (book). Original summary written for this project.

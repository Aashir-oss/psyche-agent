TITLE: Gratitude Practice
WHEN_TO_USE: When attention is locked on what's wrong and good things go unnoticed — a gentle counterweight, not a replacement for addressing real problems.
STEPS:
- Each evening, write down 3 specific good things from the day (specific beats generic: "the rain smell on my walk" > "nature").
- For one of them, write WHY it happened and who/what made it possible.
- Once a week, tell someone you appreciate them — a short message counts.
- If writing feels forced, just mentally note one good thing before sleep.
- Research shows the benefit builds over 2–4 weeks of regular practice, not from a single day.
SOURCE: Emmons, R. A. & McCullough, M. E. — research on gratitude interventions (research literature). Original summary written for this project.

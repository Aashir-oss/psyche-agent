TITLE: Thought Record (CBT)
WHEN_TO_USE: When a strong emotion follows a specific situation and you suspect an unhelpful automatic thought is driving it — e.g. anxiety before a meeting, shame after a mistake.
STEPS:
- Write down the situation in one factual sentence (who, what, where — no interpretations).
- Rate your emotion 0–100 and name it (anxious, sad, angry...).
- Catch the automatic thought: what went through your mind? Write it word for word.
- Look for evidence FOR and AGAINST the thought, like a fair judge.
- Write a balanced alternative thought that fits the evidence better.
- Re-rate the emotion 0–100 and note what changed.
SOURCE: Beck, J. S. — Cognitive Behavior Therapy: Basics and Beyond (book; chapter on cognitive restructuring). Original summary written for this project; method described in the author's own words.

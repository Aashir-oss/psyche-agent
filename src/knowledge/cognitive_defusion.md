TITLE: Cognitive Defusion (Unhooking from Thoughts)
WHEN_TO_USE: When a painful thought loops on repeat and feels like absolute truth — "I'm a failure," "Nobody cares" — and arguing with it doesn't help.
STEPS:
- Notice the thought and silently label it: "I'm having the thought that I'm a failure."
- Say it in a silly voice, or sing it to a tune — this breaks the spell of seriousness.
- Thank your mind for the thought: "Thanks, mind, I hear you." Then redirect attention to what you're doing.
- Ask: "Is following this thought helping me live the way I want right now?"
- The goal is not to eliminate the thought, but to change your relationship with it — thoughts are passing events, not facts.

ID: worry_time
TITLE: Scheduled Worry Time
WHEN_TO_USE: When worries pop up all day and hijack your focus — rumination that repeats without solving anything.
STEPS:
- Pick a fixed 15-minute "worry appointment" each day (not right before bed).
- During the day, when a worry appears, jot it in one line on paper or your phone and postpone it: "I'll deal with you at 6pm."
- At worry time, go through the list. For each worry ask: is there an action I can take? If yes, plan one small step. If no, practice letting it sit without solving.
- When time is up, close the list. New worries wait until tomorrow's appointment.
- Most people find daytime worries lose power by the evening.
SOURCE: Stimulus-control technique for worry from the research literature (Borkovec et al.); standard CBT practice. Original summary written for this project.

TITLE: Activity Scheduling
WHEN_TO_USE: When days feel empty or shapeless and mood sinks — especially during holidays, unemployment, or study breaks.
STEPS:
- On paper, block tomorrow into morning / afternoon / evening.
- Put ONE meaningful activity in each block (meaningful = matters to you, not impressive to others).
- Add one pleasant activity per day, however small (favorite tea, music, sunlight).
- Treat scheduled blocks like appointments with yourself — show up.
- At night, tick off what you did and rate the day 1–10. Watch the trend over a week.
- Empty time is where rumination breeds; a loose structure starves it.
SOURCE: Beck, A. T. et al. — Cognitive Therapy of Depression (book; activity scheduling). Original summary written for this project.

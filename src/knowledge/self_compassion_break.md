TITLE: Self-Compassion Break
WHEN_TO_USE: When self-criticism is loud — after a mistake, failure, or when you speak to yourself in ways you'd never speak to a friend.
STEPS:
- Mindfulness: say to yourself, "This is a moment of suffering." Name it honestly, without exaggerating.
- Common humanity: remind yourself, "Suffering is part of life; I'm not alone in feeling this."
- Kindness: place a hand on your chest and say what you'd say to a good friend in the same spot — e.g. "You're doing your best, and that's enough."
- Takes 1–2 minutes. Use it in the moment, not just afterwards.
- Notice the difference between harsh self-criticism (paralyzing) and kind honesty (motivating).
